Privacy Policy — Capi Sem Freio

Effective October 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the Capi Sem Freio game (Android), by Neural Pixel Play, uses, why, and how you can control it.

Summary: the game has no sign-up, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send your progress to any server of ours. Ads are served by Google AdMob and gameplay statistics are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase; both collect some device data, as described below.

1. Data the game stores on your device

Your progress (oranges, records, purchased skins, game statistics) and your settings (volume, vibration, language) are saved only on your device. Neural Pixel Play has no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner and optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to continue a run after a crash or to double the oranges from a run). These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device advertising IDServing and measuring ads; personalization, where allowed
IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)Serving region-appropriate ads; fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS, language)Compatibility and ad measurement
Ad interactions and diagnosticsMeasurement, fraud prevention and bug fixing

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Data is encrypted in transit.

3. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it, Capi Sem Freio sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as a run starting and ending, distance reached, the place in the trip, the obstacle you crashed into, tutorial steps, purchases of looks with oranges and whether a rewarded ad was watched. Along with these events, Firebase records an app instance ID (created on install, deleted on uninstall), device information (model, Android version, language) and approximate location derived from the IP address.

This data does not include your name, email or contacts, is not used to personalize ads, and is seen by Neural Pixel Play only in aggregate (for example, "how many runs reached the Seaside"). It is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy terms, encrypted in transit.

4. Consent and choices

  • Europe and the UK: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Personalized ads: in any region, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Ads keep showing, but are not personalized.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Usage statistics: you can delete the app instance ID at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings.

5. Android permissions

Internet (loading ads), vibration (on crashes, can be turned off in the game settings) and advertising ID (ads, section 2). The game does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

6. Children

Capi Sem Freio is intended for people 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

7. Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)

You may request information about, correction of, or deletion of your data. Since Neural Pixel Play does not store your personal data, requests about ad data can be made directly to Google using the tools at myaccount.google.com, or through our contact below and we will help.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
