Privacy Policy — Hocus Pogo!

Effective since November 16, 2026

This policy explains what data the game Hocus Pogo! (Android), by Neural Pixel Play, uses, why, and how you can control it.

Summary: the game has no sign-up, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send your progress to any server of ours. Ads are served by Google AdMob, purchases are made through Google Play, and gameplay statistics are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase; these Google services collect some device data, as described below.

1. Data the game keeps on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, pages, upgrades and outfits bought, records), purchases already delivered and your settings (volume, vibration, language, controls) are stored only on your device. Neural Pixel Play has no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to double a level's coins, revive where you fell or open the daily chest) and, from time to time, a full-screen ad between levels. There are no ads during a level. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device advertising IDShowing and measuring ads; personalization, when allowed
IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)Region-appropriate ads; fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS, language)Ad compatibility and measurement
Ad interactions and diagnosticsMeasurement, fraud prevention and bug fixing

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Data is transmitted encrypted.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The game sells coin packs and an option to remove the ads between levels. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: Neural Pixel Play does not receive or store your payment data (card, billing address). The game only receives the purchase confirmation from Google Play (product and a purchase code) to deliver what you bought and to restore ad removal if you reinstall the game. Everything for sale can also be earned by playing.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and improve it, Hocus Pogo! sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as starting, finishing or leaving a level, time and number of falls, the part of the level where the character fell, shop purchases with coins, whether an ad was watched and the result of an in-app purchase. Along with these events, Firebase records an app instance ID (created on install, deleted on uninstall), device information (model, Android version, language) and approximate location derived from the IP address.

This data does not include your name, email or contacts, is not used to personalize ads and is seen by Neural Pixel Play only in aggregate (for example, "which level players fall the most in"). It is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy terms, with encrypted transmission.

5. Consent and choices

  • Europe, the UK and regions that require it: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. Usage statistics are only turned on with your consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Personalized ads: in any region you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Without it, ads still appear but are not personalized.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.
  • Usage statistics: you can delete the app instance ID at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings.

6. Android permissions

Internet (ads, purchases and statistics), vibration (when taking damage and at a few game moments; can be turned off in the game settings), Google Play billing (in-app purchases) and advertising ID (ads, section 2). The game does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

7. Children

Hocus Pogo! is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

8. Your rights (LGPD and GDPR)

You may request information about the processing of your data, correction or deletion. Since Neural Pixel Play does not store your personal data, requests about ad and statistics data can be made directly to Google through the tools at myaccount.google.com, or through our contact below, and we will help.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the updated date.
