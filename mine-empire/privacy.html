Privacy Policy — Mine Empire

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy explains what data the Mine Empire game (Android), by Neural Games, uses, why, and how you can control it.

Summary: the game has no sign-up, does not ask for your name or email, and does not send your progress to any server of ours. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

1. Data the game stores on your device

Your progress (gold, upgrades, current mine, game statistics) and your settings (volume, vibration, language) are saved only on your device. Neural Games has no access to them. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or use the restart option.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner and optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one to earn gold). These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device advertising IDServing and measuring ads; personalization, where allowed
IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)Serving region-appropriate ads; fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS, language)Compatibility and ad measurement
Ad interactions and diagnosticsMeasurement, fraud prevention and bug fixing

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Data is encrypted in transit.

3. Consent and choices

  • Europe and the UK: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Personalized ads: in any region, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Ads keep showing, but are not personalized.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional.

4. Android permissions

Internet (loading ads), vibration (touch feedback, can be turned off in the game settings) and advertising ID (ads, section 2). The game does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

5. Children

Mine Empire is intended for people 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

6. Your rights (GDPR and LGPD)

You may request information about, correction of, or deletion of your data. Since Neural Games does not store your personal data, requests about ad data can be made directly to Google using the tools at myaccount.google.com, or through our contact below and we will help.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
